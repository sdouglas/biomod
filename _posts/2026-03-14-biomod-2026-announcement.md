We are thrilled to announce the schedule and venue for BIOMOD 2026!

This year, our annual jamboree will take place at **Campus Querétaro, Tecnológico de Monterrey, Mexico, from November 20 to 22**.

Team registration will be open from **April 15 to May 31**. We will release the event details and guidelines on April 15, so please keep an eye on our updates.
