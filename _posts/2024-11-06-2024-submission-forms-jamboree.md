# Call for Judges (for Mentors)

[Google Forms](https://forms.gle/89NEurNG96E8oVtH8)

Registration deadline: 2024/11/14 23:59

We need your help participating in this year's competition as a judge. The online judging period (webpage and YouTube video) will take place on Nov 15 – Nov 21 (JST), and the live presentation judging at the jamboree will be held as a hybrid style (on-site and Zoom) on Nov 22 (JST). Joining the judging online, even part of the process, is also welcomed.
