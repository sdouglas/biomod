We are happy to share the detailed information of BIOMOD 2026, held at Tecnologico de Monterrey, Querétaro campus! We look forward to receiving your team registration.

# Team Registration

- Team: 30,000 JPY (approx. 200 USD)
- Jamboree (individual): TBD (see below)

Team Registration Website (Molecular Robotics Society (Molbot) Payment System):

- English: [https://molbot.org/en/event-categories/biomod-event/18-biomod-2026-team](https://molbot.org/en/event-categories/biomod-event/18-biomod-2026-team)
- 中文: [https://molbot.org/zh/event-categories/biomod-event/18-biomod-2026-team](https://molbot.org/zh/event-categories/biomod-event/18-biomod-2026-team)
- 日本語: [https://molbot.org/ja/event-categories/biomod-event/18-biomod-2026-team](https://molbot.org/ja/event-categories/biomod-event/18-biomod-2026-team)


A fee of 30,000 JPY is due at the time of team registration, payable by Stripe or PayPal via Molbot's new payment system.
Team registration starts April 15. The registration may close early or extend due to the number cap of the jamboree venue.


**Cancellation Policy:** Please be advised that the team registration fee is non-refundable.


This year’s individual Jamboree registration fee will be decided based on the number of teams that are willing to attend on-site ($220/person in 2019). Online participation may also require an online registration fee to cover streaming etc. We strongly recommend joining on-site to experience the BIOMOD jamboree fully. Jamboree registration starts in August and is due in September, along with your project title and abstract.


Registrations are only considered complete when payment is received, and a confirmation email is sent.


# Schedule

- **Apr. 15 - Jun. 1**: Team registration (may close early / extend due to the number cap)
- **Aug. 15 - Sep. 15** (may change): Individual Jamboree registration
- **Sep. 1** Visa invitation letter requests due
- **Nov. 14**: Website/Youtube freeze
- **Nov. 20-22**: Jamboree (on-site (Querétaro, Mexico) and online (Zoom))

# Join BIOMOD Portal & Slack Community

## Portal (for all members)
We welcome all BIOMODers to join our portal website ([https://portal.biomod.jp/](https://portal.biomod.jp/)). Teams will use this platform to submit their videos, website links, and abstracts. Future important announcements regarding the jamboree will also be posted here. Judges will use this portal for the scoring process as well. Additionally, we launched the BIOMOD Archive last year, allowing members to review results from previous competitions.

## Slack (for mentors and judges)
We also invite faculty, postdoctoral mentors, and judges to join the BIOMOD Institute Slack workspace. Important announcements during the competition — as well as future discussions — will be shared here, so we highly encourage all eligible members to participate.

Let’s build a collaborative and international BIOMOD community together! Please contact [Office (BIOMOD Institute HQ)](mailto:info@biomod.jp) for details.
