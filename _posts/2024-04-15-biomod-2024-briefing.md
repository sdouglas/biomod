We are excited to announce that the team registration for this year's BIOMOD starts on **April 29, 2024**. Also, we are happy to share the news that this year's jamboree will take place in Kyushu, Japan.

An **Online briefing session** will be held on April 29 (12:00-13:00 (JST), Zoom). Rules, schedule, jamboree venue, awards, etc., will be covered. The recording will also be available later on YouTube. We look forward to seeing you all at the briefing!

# Online Briefing
- April 29, 12:00 (noon) - 13:00 (JST, Japan Standard Time)
	- [Zoom Link](https://zoom.us/j/92615962927?pwd=SGdxNzZBd3RQYVg1WEt4MS9OVTlYZz09)
	- The session will be held in English
